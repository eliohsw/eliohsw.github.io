# The First Post

Exciting news! After a whole year of construction, my website is finally completed. I’m always a fan of minimalism, and I’m so glad to see the design in my brain come to reality in every detail. I’ve put in a tremendous amount of effort building this, testing, debugging, migrating, and tweaking every single setting. So excited to show you all the beautiful little details I implemented!

I see my website as a digital representation of me. Therefore, I hope visitors feel the freshness and cleanliness of modernity in my design, and I hope I, myself, become more motivated by this happy wave of seeing my longest continuous online project come together step by step, brick by brick.

BTW, AI is super OP at frontend development. A truly powerful duo is GitHub Copilot + ChatGPT Codex. I’m actually very amazed by the improvement of AI over the past few years, and there are still more tools coming...

All in all, a short post, quite informal, but super thrilled! See you in the next post 👋
